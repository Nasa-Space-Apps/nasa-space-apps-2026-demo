# Assets (`public/`)

## Terrains (`public/models/*.stl`)

All the elevation models were supplied by the project owner (originals in their `Downloads`), from NASA 3D Resources. The names in `public/models` are short; the original is shown below. They are **height maps** (one Z value per X/Y) in **Z-up**, exported from Blender; the code rotates them to Y-up and centres them.

| File | Original | Size | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `terrain.stl` | `Opportunity Landing Site.stl` | 2.7 MB | Mars terrain (**Opportunity**; also **Spirit** as a stand-in for Gusev and **InSight** on its flat plain, as a stand-in for Elysium Planitia) |
| `moon-terrain.stl` | `Apollo 14 - Landing Site.stl` | 6.6 MB | Moon terrain (**Surveyor 3**; the SEV's stand-in) |

Processing: see "Terrain" in [architecture.md](architecture.md). The border flattening modifies the height in the last `fade` units of each side.

**Scale:** they are not metres. Mars ≈ 470 m/unit (≈ 263 × 356 km) and the Moon ≈ 118 m/unit (≈ 30 × 30 km), both with an exaggerated vertical axis. See "Unresolved: real scale" in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md). **Useful features** (centred world coordinates; `X = x_stl − centre`, `Z = −(y_stl − centre)`): in both, the **centre is a crater** (the landing point; on Mars with very steep walls). Mars: a big depression towards (205, −170) and a low area to the south-east; high ground to the west (X ≈ −300). Moon: a high plateau to the east/south-east (X > 20, Z > 0), low ground to the west.

## The stories (`public/models/<mission>/`)

One folder per story: `opportunity`, `spirit`, `ingenuity`, `surveyor3`, `insight`, `beagle2`. Each holds:

- `story.json` — the 20 beats (copy, facts, sources, 3D route), generated by `npm run story -- <mission>`;
- `images/<id>.jpg` — 20 AI illustrations at 1040 px, 4:5 (≈ 3.6–4.0 MB per story; opportunity ≈ 4 MB), plus `manifest.json` with each image's prompt and prediction (Nano Banana 2.1 on Replicate); the 1856×2304 originals live outside the repo (`~/Downloads/images/<mission>/`);
- `audio/<id>.mp3` + `manifest.json` — the narrations (voice Bill, ≈ 6.6–8 MB per story), recorded with `npm run story-audio -- <mission>`.

The flow and the recipe are in [story.md](story.md). Note: the mission id of `beagle-2` and `surveyor-3` has a hyphen but their folders do not.

## Other public assets

- `public/guide/` — 30 mp3 + `manifest.json`: the **generic voice guide** (distance, progress, stopped, ready, end) and Opportunity's phrases; voice Daniel, `eleven_flash_v2_5`. See [guide.md](guide.md).
- `public/ambient/` — the 3 background beds (`moon`, `mars`, `timeline`) + `manifest.json`. See [ambient.md](ambient.md).
- `public/team/` — `jackson.jpg` and `paul.jpg`: flat illustrated portraits (4:5, 720 × 894 px, ≈ 22 and 27 KB) supplied by the owner (the 1856 × 2304 originals are in their downloads). Used by the "created by" card and the authors' line of every closing card (`lib/team.ts` → `portraitOf`).
- `public/story/` — standalone HTML viewers of the research team's Opportunity prototypes (`historia.html`, `juego2.html`, `juego2_mapa.html`), generated by `story/build_*.py`: see [`story/README.md`](../story/README.md). Nothing in the app links to them.

## The globe

There are no assets: the globes are a grid of lines generated by code (`Globe.tsx`, in orthographic projection). Realistic textures (Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0) were tried and dropped for style.

## Vehicles

**No GLB is used**: the vehicles are **procedural** (`src/lib/vehicles/`), in the flat 3-tone style of [design.md](design.md#vehicles), with proportions measured on NASA's GLB models (the GLBs and reference photos were removed from the repo; where they came from and under what licence is in [model-sources.md](model-sources.md)). Measures in scene units (not metres; they stay oversized, see [roadmap.md](roadmap.md)):
