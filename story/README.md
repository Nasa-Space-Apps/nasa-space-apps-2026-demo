# Opportunity: story viewer and decision game (research-team prototypes)

Two narrative products about the Opportunity mission (MER-B), written for secondary-school students. They are standalone HTML pages (with the JSON embedded), served from `public/story/`, and they do **not** depend on the rest of the web app. They are the research team's prototypes; the shipped product is the demo described in [`docs/story.md`](../docs/story.md). The game is the design source for the future **Decisions mode** ([`docs/game-design.md`](../docs/game-design.md)); it is not built into the demo.

> The content (titles, narration, UI of the viewers) is in Spanish on purpose: it is research data of a Spanish-speaking team.

| Product | JSON (source) | HTML | URL with `npm run dev` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Story: 20 events in 5 acts | `data/historia_opportunity.json` | `public/story/historia.html` | `/story/historia.html` |
| Decision game v2: 30 nodes, 6 decisions | `data/juego2_opportunity.json` | `public/story/juego2.html` (playable prototype) and `juego2_mapa.html` (graph map, internal tool) | `/story/juego2.html` |

(An earlier 30-node game, v1, written before the facts were verified, was removed: v2 replaces it.)

## Data and regeneration

- `data/linea_de_tiempo_opportunity.json` is the source of the story: the 81 events with sources. The story picks events from it by id (`opp001`…).
- The game uses the **verified dossiers** (`misiones/opportunity/dossiers/<id>/dossier.json`) instead.
- `build_historia.py` and `build_juego2.py` hold the selection and the editorial writing, regenerate the JSON and write the HTML from `templates/`:

```
python story/build_historia.py
python story/build_juego2.py
python -m unittest discover -s story/tests          # 19 tests: structure, traceability, no unconfirmed claims
```

- Each build also writes `data/<name>_opportunity.js` (`window.DATOS = …`). The templates in `templates/` load it with a `<script src="../data/…">` tag, so **they open with a double click (`file://`)** or over http and show the data without generating anything. The final HTML in `public/story/` has the data embedded and does not need that file.

## The decision game v2 (`data/juego2_opportunity.json`)

This JSON is the **single source** of the game "Opportunity: tú decides" (30 nodes) and of its cards. It is generated by `build_juego2.py`; **do not edit it by hand**.

### Content rules (mandatory)

1. **The 18 real nodes may only assert what is in `respaldo_detalle`.** Do not add facts, dates, figures or quotes.
2. **The 12 fictional nodes** (`ficticio: true`) are narrated as if they had happened, but **without sources, quotes or figures**. The interface must show them as an "alternative line".
3. When a fictional branch ends (level 2), `leccion` ("what really happened") is always shown and the player **goes back to the decision**.
4. Every **decision** must show `contexto.lo_que_sabes` and `contexto.en_juego` before the options.

### Structure

| Top-level key | Contents |
|---|---|
| `nodos[]` | The 30 nodes. |
| `aristas[]` | The 35 edges of the graph: `continuar`, `opcion_correcta`, `opcion_equivocada`, `volver_a_decidir`. |
| `capitulos[]` | The 5 chapters. |
| `inicio` | Id of the first node (`n01`). |
| `diccionario` | What each field means. |
| `uso_para_agentes` | The rules above. |

| `tipo` | `rol` | Count | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| `historia` | `historia_real` | 12 | Verified real narrative. |
| `decision` | `decision` | 6 | Situation, context, question and 2 options (one real, one wrong). |
| `escena_alternativa` | `ficticio_consecuencia` | 6 | Level 1 of the wrong branch: the consequence, narrated as real. |
| `fallo` | `ficticio_final_alternativo` | 6 | Level 2: the outcome; includes `leccion` and goes back to the decision (`volver_a`). |

The real path is the 18 nodes with `orden_camino_real` 1 to 18. Each wrong option opens exactly 2 fictional nodes. **The node arithmetic:** 30 nodes = 18 real + 12 fictional (6 decisions × 1 wrong option × 2 levels); a decision with 3 options would not fit.

Main node fields: `id`, `tipo`, `rol`, `camino` (`real` | `alternativo`), `ficticio`, `capitulo`, `titulo`, `fecha`; `narrativa.texto`, `narrativa.escena_visual`, `narrativa.emocion`; for decisions `contexto`, `pregunta`, `opciones[]` (`id`, `texto`, `detalle`, `correcta`, `siguiente`) and `resultado_real`; for level-2 fictional nodes `leccion`; for fictional nodes `rama_de`, `opcion_origen` and `nivel`; `dato_desbloqueado` (`titulo`, `texto`) on real nodes; and traceability: `respaldo` (references such as `opp009#3` = fact 3 of the `datos_reales` of the dossier `opp009`) with `respaldo_detalle[]` (the verified claim, the literal quote, the URL and where it is used).

### The six decisions

`n03` one or two rovers · `n05` where to land · `n07` the battery that failed at the last moment · `n11` stuck in the sand (Purgatory Dune) · `n13` heading for a distant crater · `n17` the silence after the storm. Each one separates "what the team knew" from "what happened", and the options do not give the answer away (each carries an advantage and a cost).

## Content notes

- The story's facts and sources come from the timeline; the narration fields (`gancho`, `escena_visual`) are editorial writing.
- Nodes with `hipotetico: true` are imagined scenarios that did not happen and are shown labelled as such; the correct option of each decision matches reality.
- `opp001` (conception of the rover): the research agent could not confirm "geological boots on the ground" nor "warm and wet or cold and dry", and 1987 is the start of four proposals, not the approval year. The text of `opp001` and node `n01` is still to be corrected.
